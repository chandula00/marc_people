Mahela Pandukabhaya is currently a PhD candidate at the Faculty of Engineering, University of Peradeniya, specializing in wearable technologies, biomechanical motion capture, and psychomotor skill analysis. His research interests lie at the intersection of electrical and electronic engineering, biomedical engineering, signal processing, and intelligent sensing systems, with a particular focus on developing wearable technologies for human movement analysis and personalized healthcare.

Mahela has been actively involved in research at MARC since 2020 as a volunteer researcher. His work encompasses the development of wearable sensing systems, motion estimation algorithms, and data-driven approaches to human movement analysis. In addition, he also has experience in university-level engineering education, laboratory development, and academic instruction, with a particular interest in making engineering concepts accessible through practical, hands-on learning.

Further information about his research, projects, and professional activities is available on his [personal website](https://mahela-pandukabhaya.github.io).
