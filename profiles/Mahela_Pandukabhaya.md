I am a PhD candidate at the Faculty of Engineering, University of Peradeniya, specializing in wearable technologies, biomechanical motion capture, and psychomotor skill analysis. My research interests lie at the intersection of electrical and electronic engineering, biomedical engineering, signal processing, and intelligent sensing systems, with a particular focus on developing wearable technologies for human movement analysis and personalized healthcare.

I have been actively involved in research at the Motion Analysis Research Centre (MARC) since 2020, initially as a volunteer researcher. My work encompasses the development of wearable sensing systems, motion estimation algorithms, and data-driven approaches to human movement analysis. I also have experience in university-level engineering education, laboratory development, and academic instruction, with a particular interest in making engineering concepts accessible through practical, hands-on learning.

Further information about my research, projects, and professional activities is available on my [personal website](https://mahela-pandukabhaya.github.io).
