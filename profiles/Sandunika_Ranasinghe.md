Sandunika Ranasinghe is an Electrical and Electronic Engineering graduate with First Class Honours from the University of Peradeniya, Sri Lanka. Her research lies at the intersection of artificial intelligence, image and signal processing, computer vision, and applied mathematics. Her research interests include deep learning, generative modelling, diffusion models, implicit neural representations, multispectral imaging, and the development of theoretically grounded machine learning methodologies with practical applicability.

At MARC, her research encompasses diffusion-based image and audio generation, implicit neural representations for image restoration and colourisation, multispectral imaging for agricultural and environmental monitoring, and vision-language models for long-video understanding. Her research on diffusion models focuses on investigating the mathematical and statistical properties of the diffusion process and developing theoretically motivated regularisation techniques to enhance structural coherence and fidelity in generative image synthesis. Her work in computer vision explores query-guided frame selection to improve the effectiveness of long-video understanding and question answering.
